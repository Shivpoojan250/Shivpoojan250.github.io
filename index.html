Electric Vehicle Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard on about 150K electric vehicles, showing adoption by year, state, make, model and clean-fuel incentive eligibility.

  • BEVs make up 78% of vehicles and PHEVs 22%, with an average electric range of 67.83 km.
  • Tesla leads with 69K vehicles, and Model Y (29K) and Model 3 (28K) are the top models.
  • About 46% of vehicles are eligible for clean alternative fuel incentives.

Telecom Customer Churn Analysis & Prediction

Analyzed telecom customer data to find who churns and why, then built models to predict it.

  • 1,869 customers churned (about 26%), putting roughly $3.7M of revenue at risk.
  • Month-to-month contracts churn far more, and fiber-optic customers bring 58.9% of revenue.
  • Logistic Regression and Random Forest models rank the top churn drivers.

Retail Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

Studied 3,900 purchase records to understand what drives spending and to segment shoppers.

  • Subscribers spend 68% more than non-subscribers and repurchase 78% of the time.
  • Found a "Smart Shopper" segment: discount-driven, with above-average spend.
  • K-Means clustering segments customers, and Linear Regression predicts purchase amount.

Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

Analyzed 4,117 orders to track sales growth and find where profit is lost.

  • $2.35M in sales and $283K in profit, with 82.3% sales growth from 2011 to 2014.
  • Tables lose money (-23.2% margin), and discounts correlate negatively with profit (-0.22).
  • A Linear Regression model predicts profit and shows the effect of discounts.
